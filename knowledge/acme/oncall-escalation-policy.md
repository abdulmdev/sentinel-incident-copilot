# On-call escalation policy

## Severity levels
- SEV1: customer-facing outage or data loss. Page the incident commander immediately; status page update within 15 minutes.
- SEV2: degraded service with a workaround. Page the owning team.
- SEV3: internal impact only. Ticket, handle in business hours.

## Escalation
If the primary on-call doesn't acknowledge within 5 minutes, the page goes to the secondary, then to the engineering manager after 15 minutes.
